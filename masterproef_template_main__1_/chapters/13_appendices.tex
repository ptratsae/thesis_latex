\section*{LLM Labelling Prompt and Example Output}
\label{app:llm_prompt}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{LLM Labelling Prompt and Example Output}
This appendix provides the full prompt used to generate recruiter-side
suitability labels via the Qwen3.5~(35B) model, together with a
representative example of the model's JSON output. The design is discussed in
Section~\ref{sec:exp_recruiter_datagen}.

\subsection*{Prompt}

{\footnotesize
\begin{verbatim}
Task: You are an HR recruiter. Evaluate how well each person fits the
job based on their profile and the job description.
(Important: Treat each job-user pair independently.)

Assign a fit level:
- "Very Good Fit": Meets 100% of Must-Haves AND 50%+ of Nice-to-Haves
  AND 0 Deal-Breakers.
- "Good Fit": Meets 100% of Must-Haves (OR misses 1 Must-Have but
  compensates with extensive relevant experience) AND 0 Deal-Breakers.
- "Poor Fit": Missing 2+ Must-Haves OR has exactly 1 Deal-Breaker.
- "Very Poor Fit": Missing 3+ Must-Haves OR has 2+ Deal-Breakers.

Return a JSON object:
{"results": [{"candidate_id": <id>, "must_haves": "...",
  "nice_to_haves": "...", "deal_breakers": "...",
  "reasoning": "...", "fit_level": "Good Fit"}]}

Notes:
- Be strict. Only candidates that truly meet requirements get Good Fit.
- Be objective. Focus on concrete matches/mismatches.
- If Degree, Major, Experience or History is empty/NaN, assume the
  candidate does not have that feature.
- Assume all applicants are 18+, available, and physically able.

Job Description: {job_desc}
Candidate Profiles: {profiles_text}
\end{verbatim}
}

ensuring the reasoning is traceable and the label is not assigned

      "fit_level": "Poor Fit"

\section*{Hybrid Congestion Ablation}
\label{app:hybrid_ablation}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Hybrid Congestion Ablation}

Tables~\ref{tab:app_hybrid_job} and~\ref{tab:app_hybrid_candidate} extend 
the results of Section~\ref{sec:exp_hybrid_results} with the two intermediate 
universes. On the job side, Universes~1 and~3 are identical, as are 
Universes~2 and~4 --- the candidate-side penalty operates only in Phase~2 
and has no effect on which jobs users apply to. On the candidate side, all 
four universes differ. Notably, the job-side penalty alone (Universe~2) 
reduces candidate congestion further than the candidate-side penalty alone 
(Universe~3): Gini 0.5253 vs.\ 0.5765. This confirms that redistributing 
the application pool in Phase~1 is a more powerful lever for candidate equity 
than re-ranking within fixed inboxes in Phase~2.
