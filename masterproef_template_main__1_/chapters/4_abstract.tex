\textbf{Title:} Can AI help you find your dream job?\\

This master's dissertation studies how AI and recommender systems can improve matching quality in a two-sided job market. More specifically, the research is focused on developing and evaluating a recommendation system which not only considers the preferences of job seekers but also the requirements of recruiters. The goal is reducing the congestion while maintaining fair and efficient matching. The work proposes a user-side recommender system based on a two-tower model architecture for job recommendation and a recruiter-side lightweight logistic regression model for candidate suitability ranking. The data pipeline combines preprocessing and generating synthetic data for the lightweight model as no data was available for training. Finally a hybrid congestion balancing strategy is proposed by first analyzing the congestion on both sides of the market and then implementing a combined strategy to reduce over-exposure of popular jobs and fair competition among users.
