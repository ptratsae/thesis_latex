\textbf{Title:} Can AI help you find your dream job?\\
\textbf{Author:} Pharrell Tratsaert\\

This dissertation studies how AI can improve matching quality in a two-sided job market by reducing congestion and improving recommendation relevance for both job seekers and recruiters. The work proposes a two-way recommendation framework consisting of a user-side \gls{RS} based on a two-tower model for job recommendation and a recruiter-side lightweight logistic regression model for candidate suitability ranking. The data pipeline combines preprocessing and generating synthetic data for the lightweight model as no data was available for training. Finally a hybrid congestion balancing strategy is proposed by first analyzing the congestion on both sides of the market and then implementing a combined strategy to reduce over-exposure of popular jobs and fair competition among users.
