\textbf{Title:} Can AI help you find your dream job?\\
\textbf{Author:} Pharrell Tratsaert\\

\textbf{Academic year:} 2025-2026
This dissertation studies how AI-driven recommender systems can improve matching quality in a two-sided job market by reducing congestion while preserving fair and efficient matches. A dual-phase approach is proposed: a user-side recommender based on a Two-Tower neural architecture that generates candidate job shortlists for jobseekers, and a recruiter-side lightweight logistic regression model that scores candidate suitability for shortlisting. Because recruiter training labels were not available, we develop a preprocessing pipeline that synthesizes training examples and produces a compact recruiter model for fast inference. We combine these components in a hybrid congestion-balancing strategy that applies job-level adjustments at recommendation time and candidate-level adjustments during recruiter shortlisting, evaluated via counterfactual simulations on real application logs. Metrics include applications-per-job and shortlists-per-candidate distributions, Gini coefficients, and match quality (model-based probabilities). Results show the combined strategy can meaningfully reduce over-exposure of popular jobs and rebalance recruiter attention with minimal loss of matching relevance. The work demonstrates that coordinating user-side recommendations and recruiter-side ranking is an effective lever for congestion mitigation in large-scale labor platforms.
